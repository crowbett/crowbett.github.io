# Design

How the site looks and behaves, and why. Every Design-track task in `docs/tasks.md` cites a section here; a change that contradicts this file updates it in the same commit.

## Feel

**An instrument panel.** Cold paper, black rules, one signal blue, a dark screen that shows real output, and lamps that only light when something is live. The page reads like a piece of test equipment: labelled, ruled, and exact.

A visitor should read it as the work of someone who measures before claiming: data set like data, nothing decorative that is not also information, and a page that is fast and steady on a cheap phone. It must not read as a default AI-built page: 2026-10-26 research found the previous look (cream paper, terracotta accent, uppercase mono eyebrows, identical cards, "→" links) listed almost item for item as a tell in Anthropic's own frontend-design skill (`~/research/reports/Striking engineer portfolio design.md`).

## Type

Four self-hosted OFL faces, 21,784 bytes of woff2 in total (measured), each pinned to one instance so no variable-axis tables ship:

- `--display`: Archivo Expanded, Archivo pinned at `wdth 125, wght 800` (7,272 B). Headings, the wordmark, row names. Tracking −0.03em on headings, −0.05em at h1 and the wordmark.
- `--sans`: Archivo at `wdth 100, wght 400` (7,296 B). Prose, ledes, buttons in the demo mount. No bold cut ships; emphasis comes from colour and the display face.
- `--mono`: Martian Mono at `wdth 100, wght 400` (5,040 B). Labels, metrics, tags, keys, demo chrome. Lowercase labels at `--t-label`, no tracking, no uppercase.
- `--dots`: Doto at `ROND 0, wght 800` (2,176 B), a dot-matrix face for instrument numerals only: the replay readouts and the 404 code.

Subset for every face: `U+0020-007E,U+00A0,U+00A9,U+00B1,U+00B5,U+00B7,U+00D7,U+2013,U+2014,U+2018,U+2019,U+201C,U+201D,U+2026,U+2190-2193,U+2212,U+2264,U+2265`, layout features `kern, tnum, case`, no hinting, name records kept. All four are preloaded with `font-display: optional`, so none swaps in late and shifts layout; a slow first load shows the system fallbacks for that page. Licences ship beside them (`web/assets/fonts/OFL-*.txt`). `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` wherever numbers sit in columns or update live.

## Space, layout, radius

- Spacing steps (px): 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Nothing off-scale.
- Page gutter 16 px on phones, 32 px from 48rem; content max 80rem; sections 64 px apart on phones, 96 px from 48rem.
- Structure is drawn with rules, not cards: a 2 px `--rule` under the header, under section heads and over the footer; 1 px rules between index rows and around modules.
- Radius 3 px (`--r-1`) on modules and keys, 2 px on inputs and tags, round only on lamps. No soft shadows: keys carry a hard 2 px offset shadow in `--rule` (accent-hover on filled keys) that disappears as the key presses.

## Motion

- Durations: 120 ms for colour feedback, 200 ms for state changes and key travel, never above 300 ms for UI.
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` entering, `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` leaving, and `--spring`, a `linear()` curve with ~12% overshoot, for key travel only (press in 40 ms, spring back in 200 ms).
- Animate `opacity` and `transform` only; nothing that moves layout.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` turns every transition and animation off, and demos do not auto-play.
- No scroll-triggered reveals of content that should already be readable; no page-load stagger.
- **Diagram motion** (samwho's moving thumbnails): each diagram's accent element acts out its mechanism: Raft leaders pulse and heartbeats march (quorum, keel), bars rise to the limit and only then does the denied overflow grow above it, and after "redis down" the local limit draws in higher (ratelimitd), three clients' requests meet at the cache, one fetch goes to origin while the other two wait, and the answer returns to all three (edgecache, 4 s), the active segment fills (kvlite), a third worker's claim passes the two locked rows and locks the next free one (jobrun, 3.2 s), the queried function pulses (codegraph), the nozzle sweeps the layer (strata). It plays once when a row's or the facts column's drawing is 60% on screen (web/assets/motion.js), and loops while a row is hovered or keyboard-focused. Every keyframe starts and ends on the static drawing, so a screenshot, a print or reduced motion always shows the finished picture. No keyframe scales to exactly 0: on a `non-scaling-stroke` shape that singular transform froze a visitor's Mac (F6), so shapes grow from `scale(0.001)` and hide with `opacity: 0`; `site.Check` rejects a zero scale in any built CSS, HTML or JS.
- **Checks:** e2e measures CLS at load on every page (must be 0) and counts `document.getAnimations()` with and without reduced motion on home, a project page and the smoke demo.

## Home

What it is for: in one look, who this is, what they build, and that the proof is runnable.

- **Wordmark and line**: the name in the display face, sized so it spans the content width at every viewport (`min(80rem, 100vw − 2 × gutter) / 4.45`), then the hero sentence in `--text-2` at lede size ("builds databases, … and measured benchmarks"); both sit in one `h1`, so the heading reads as the sentence. The role line ("Software engineer (Go, Rust)") comes before the `h1`: on phones it labels the wordmark from above, like a model plate; from 64rem the hero is a 12-column grid, the `h1` a subgrid over its first two rows, and the role sits in columns 1-5 on the sentence's baseline while the sentence and hook take 6-12.
- **Hook and keys**: one line that says how many projects run live, then one key per live demo, named by title, straight to that page's `#demo`.
- **Replay** (the instrument): one real run of ratelimitd's GCRA (`race`, attack at 10× the limit, seed 1) recorded from the WebAssembly build into `data/hero-trace.json` and drawn at build time on a dark graticule screen, wide 480×200 from 40rem and tall 320×240 below it. Denied (`--deny`) above, allowed (`--screen-ok`) below, each lane on its own scale with a labelled reference (the dashed peak line, "526/s peak", and the orange limit line, "50/s limit"); the attack window is shaded `--attack` and labelled "10× attack". A screen-coloured rect wipes off the bars over 1.8 s, and the labels fade in as it passes them (`.f1`–`.f5`). Beside the screen (below it on phones) three readouts in Doto: allowed (in `--live`), denied and limit, all from the trace. Under it "Run it in your browser", the page's one filled key: on click, and only then, `web/assets/hero.js` loads the build, makes the recorded call, compares the whole answer with the recording, replays the wipe, lights a "60/60 buckets identical in your browser" lamp and offers "Try your own limit and attack". Without JS the button is inert and the chart reads the same; e2e `TestHeroRun` clicks it and requires the match, and `TestHeroTrace` re-runs the call through the binary on every deploy (docs/demo-contract.md §Home replay).
- **Work index**: "Work" with the project and live counts on the right, counted at build time, then one ruled row per project, live projects first. From 52rem a row is four cells, divided by hairlines: the diagram on a dot grid, the name with its language, the tagline and metric, and the action cell (a lit "Live demo" lamp and a "Run it" key, or an unlit "Source on request/GitHub" lamp and a "Details" key). On phones the diagram and name share a line and the body and action stack under them. The whole row is one link (to `#demo` when live); hover and keyboard focus fill it with the signal blue, invert its text, drawing and key, and loop its diagram.
- **Diagrams** (`templates/diagrams.html`): one inline SVG line drawing per slug, viewBox 240×120, 1.5 px strokes that stay 1.5 px at any size (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`), one accent element that names the idea. Labels (`.t`) show only in the project page's facts column, where they are legible. A project with no drawing keeps its empty cell so the columns line up.

## Project page

What it is for: a visitor checks the claim, then runs it.

- **Header**: crumb (`Work / slug`), the title at `--t-h1` in the display face, the tagline as lede, closed by a 2 px rule.
- **Facts** (a spec sheet): a ruled module with the project's diagram on a dot grid, then `language`, `topics`, `demo` (only when live: a lit lamp linking to the frame) and `source` (repo or "Private, on request" linking to the mailto line at the foot). From 64rem it is a sticky 17rem column left of the text.
- **Evidence**: a module whose black plate reads "Evidence", the metric in mono, its source in sans below. "Evidence", not "Measured": keel's metric is a gate, not a measurement.
- **Body** at `--measure`; the source line closes the column. On a page with a live demo the evidence module and the demo frame span the whole column; without a demo the evidence module stays at the measure.

## Demo frame

One frame for every demo, built by the site (`templates/project.html`, `web/assets/demo.js`); the demo's `ui.js` only fills the mount.

- **Title plate:** a black plate across the top of the frame: a green lamp and "Live demo" on the left; on the right the runtime and transfer size (`WebAssembly, ~1,215 KB`, gzip bytes from the lock). The project name is already the h1, so it is not repeated.
- **Instruction:** the project's `demo_try` line, under the plate, before the button (ciechanow.ski's one sentence that says what to do).
- **Before start:** the start key (filled signal blue) and one honest sentence: runs in your browser, nothing downloads until you start it.
- **Loading:** the worker streams the fetch and reports decompressed bytes. GitHub Pages sends .wasm gzipped, so Content-Length is the compressed size and useless for progress; the page compares received bytes with the raw size from the lock (`data-bytes`) and shows a determinate bar and a percentage, then "Starting…" while the binary instantiates. The button reads "Loading…"; the note gives way to the bar. Without `data-bytes` (the smoke page) the bar is indeterminate and counts KB. Screen readers get one status, not every percent.
- **Running:** the mount, full frame width; the bar and note go away. Focus moves to the mount, so Tab reaches the first control. Mount buttons wrap their label instead of overflowing on phones.
- **Error:** the status becomes a `--danger-tint` panel above the button, "The demo failed to start." in `--danger` then the message verbatim. The start button turns into "Try again" and takes focus; it starts a fresh worker.
- **No demo yet:** no frame. The page is evidence plus prose, and the facts column leaves `demo` out.

## What we will not do

No photo, illustration, mascot, emoji section markers or stock icons. No cream paper, warm-clay accents, uppercase tracked eyebrows, identical card grids or "→" appended to links (the AI-default look this direction replaced). No gradients as decoration, glass, glow, blur or noise textures. No scroll-jacking, parallax, custom cursors, typing or scramble effects, or carousels. No skill bars, logo walls, social links or follower counts. No third-party anything (fonts, analytics, embeds). No layout that needs JavaScript; with scripts off every page reads correctly and only the demo buttons are inert. No dark-only or light-only design.
